The attribution MUST be accurate and MUST NOT falsely imply that the original author endorses, sponsors, or assumes responsibility for Your work.

5.3. Prominence. Attribution MUST be placed in a location reasonably visible to the recipients or users of the Covered Work. For software distributed through a graphical user interface, the attribution MUST appear in the application’s About, Credits, Legal Notices, or equivalent interface, and MUST be accessible without unreasonable effort. For source-code repositories and source distributions, the attribution MUST appear in the root-level documentation, such as a README or NOTICE file, and in relevant source-code copyright notices where reasonably practicable.

For a network-accessible service, the attribution MUST be displayed in a publicly accessible legal notices, credits, or equivalent page linked from the service’s primary interface. If the service has no graphical interface, the attribution MUST be included in its public documentation or another prominent, publicly accessible notice.

5.4. Preservation of Notices. You MUST retain all existing copyright notices, license notices, attribution notices, and disclaimers contained in the Original Work, except where a notice does not apply to the material being distributed or removal is required by law.
